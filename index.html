Building Confident Readers: Strengthening Foundational Skills and Moving Beyond Simple Word Patterns

Every confident reader begins with strong foundations. Discover research-based, practical strategies to help children grow from recognizing simple sounds to reading with fluency and deep understanding.

What You'll Learn

Participants will explore practical, research-based strategies to strengthen children's foundational reading skills. We will specifically address how to support struggling readers who remain stuck at basic decoding stages, such as CVC (e.g., cat, dog) or CCVC (e.g., ship, frog) patterns, helping them transition into fluent, meaningful reading.

Key Takeaways

  • Understand the core components of foundational reading skills and how they build strong readers.
  • Identify why children get stuck at basic word patterns and bridge that gap effectively.
  • Learn classroom- and home-friendly strategies that make decoding, blending, and fluency natural.
  • Leave with practical tools and renewed confidence to support emerging and struggling readers.

Who Should Attend?

  • Preschool and Elementary Teachers
  • Early Childhood Educators (ECEs)
  • Literacy Coaches
  • Parents seeking structured reading support for their children
